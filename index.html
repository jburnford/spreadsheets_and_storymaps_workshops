HIST 243: Reverberations of the Industrial Revolution University of Saskatchewan

Workshops: Spreadsheets and StoryMaps

These self-paced workshops teach the two technical skills behind the course project: building a chart from historical data (Milestone 4) and building a map and exhibit in ArcGIS StoryMaps (Milestones 3 and 7). Try each step yourself first. Hints, screenshots, and a walkthrough video are there when you get stuck.

Spreadsheets workshop

Filter, sort, write a formula, and build a properly labelled chart from Britain's import statistics, 1856–1906. Excel and Google Sheets. About 90 minutes.
